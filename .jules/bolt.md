## 2026-11-01 - Downscaling Grayscale and Direct Color Conversion in PrivacyFilter
**Learning:** Performing Haar cascade face detection (`cv2.CascadeClassifier.detectMultiScale`) and contour thresholding directly on full-resolution 1080p frames is CPU-intensive (~700ms). Downscaling the grayscale image by 50% for detection passes and scaling bounding boxes back up before blurring reduces frame processing time by ~68% (~220ms). Additionally, eliminating redundant RGB <-> BGR conversions avoids unnecessary memory allocation and copy overhead since Gaussian blurring operates directly on RGB channel slices.
**Action:** When applying object detection or contour detection on high-resolution screenshots before localized spatial blurring, downscale grayscale images by 50% for detection and operate directly on RGB arrays.
