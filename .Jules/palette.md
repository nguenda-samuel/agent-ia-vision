## 2025-05-18 - CustomTkinter Icon-Only Button Accessibility
**Learning:** CustomTkinter controls lack native ARIA attributes; icon-only buttons (like `🚀` or `📸`) provide poor accessibility and visual clarity for users relying on screen readers or clear text labels.
**Action:** Always include clear text alongside icons (e.g., `🚀 Envoyer`, `📸 Écran`) on action buttons in CustomTkinter interfaces.
