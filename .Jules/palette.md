# Palette's Journal

## 2026-03-30 - CTkToolTip for Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only action buttons (`📸` and `🚀`) in CustomTkinter lack accessible tooltips or descriptive text by default, confusing first-time users.
**Action:** Always wrap icon-only CTkButtons with a custom `CTkToolTip` component to ensure instant feedback on hover and keyboard clarity.
