# Palette's Journal - Critical Learnings

## 2025-05-18 - Tooltips for CustomTkinter Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** CustomTkinter icon-only buttons (such as `📸` and `🚀`) lack visual labels or tooltips by default, making their function unclear to users who rely on visual hints or hover descriptions.
**Action:** Use `CTkToolTip` on icon-only buttons across CustomTkinter windows to provide accessible hover feedback and explicit action context.
