## 2026-08-30 - Hover Tooltips for Icon-Only Buttons in CustomTkinter
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like 📸 and 🚀) lack inline descriptive labels, making hover tooltips essential for user clarity, accessibility, and shortcut discovery (e.g. F9).
**Action:** Always wrap icon-only buttons with a `ToolTip` helper in Tkinter/CustomTkinter applications.
