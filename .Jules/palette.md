## 2026-10-14 - CustomTkinter Button Tooltips
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like 🚀 or 📸) in CustomTkinter lack built-in tooltips or hover titles, reducing clarity for screen reader users and visual mouse users.
**Action:** Attach a custom lightweight Tkinter Toplevel tooltip window on `<Enter>`/`<Leave>` events to clarify icon button actions.
